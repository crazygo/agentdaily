The Memory Shortage and AI: HBM Impact on Consumer Electronics

Simon highlighted David Oks' explanation of how AI data centers are causing memory shortages. With only three major memory manufacturers and fixed wafer capacity, HBM (high-bandwidth memory for GPUs) allocation is increasing from 2% to 20% by end of 2026. Since HBM consumes 3x more wafer capacity per GB than DDR/LPDDR, this is causing consumer electronics to become more expensive.
