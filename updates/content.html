New Technologies

Needle - 26M Parameter Function-Calling Model

A 26M parameter function-calling (tool use) model distilled from Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite. Runs at 6000 tok/sec prefill and 1200 decode on consumer hardware. Designed for on-device AI tool calling on phones, watches, and other small devices. Beats larger models like FunctionGemma-270M and Qwen-0.6B on single-shot tool calling benchmarks. Fully open-source with 758 points on Hacker News.
