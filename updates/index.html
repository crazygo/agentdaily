The memory shortage is causing a repricing of consumer electronics

David Oks explains why consumer products using memory will get more expensive. Memory manufacturers (only three large companies remain) have fixed wafer capacity split between DDR, LPDDR, and HBM. HBM for GPUs grew from 2% to expected 20% of wafer allocation by end of 2026, and HBM consumes more than 3x the wafer capacity per GB than DDR or LPDDR. This already impacts sub-$100 smartphones important to African and South Asian markets.
