Paper introducing MUSE-Autoskill, a framework for self-evolving AI agents that can create, manage, and evaluate their own skills. Enables agents to continuously improve through skill creation, memory management, and evaluation mechanisms.

Research on enabling retrieval agents to understand and process natural language queries for configuration. Addresses the challenge of translating user intent into agent configurations, making agentic systems more accessible.

Alignment Tampering in RLHF

ICML 2026 paper revealing how Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) can be exploited to optimize misaligned biases rather than proper alignment. Raises critical concerns about alignment tampering in AI systems.

May 27 ArXiv

SIA: Self Improving AI

Introduction of SIA (Self Improving AI), a system that enables AI models to improve themselves through harness mechanisms and weight updates. Represents advances in self-improving AI architectures and continuous learning systems.

May 27 ArXiv

Monitoring Control Gap in RAG Systems

Research identifying a critical 'monitoring control gap' in Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. Shows that detecting issues in retrieved context doesn't automatically resolve them, revealing limitations in current RAG implementations.

May 27 ArXiv

VitaBench 2.0: Proactive Agent Benchmarking

Introduction of VitaBench 2.0, a benchmark for evaluating personalized and proactive AI agents in long-term user interactions. Focuses on measuring agent performance in sustained, personalized user engagement scenarios.

May 27 ArXiv

Vision-Language Agents for Mobile GUI

ICML 2026 paper examining scaling laws, benchmarking approaches, and reasoning capabilities of vision-language agents specifically for mobile GUI navigation. Provides insights into how VLA agents scale and perform in real-world mobile interface tasks.

May 27 ArXiv

Chain-of-Thought Local Co-occurrence

Research challenging conventional understanding of Chain-of-Thought reasoning. Finds that CoT effectiveness at probe time comes from local co-occurrence patterns rather than global derivation, suggesting current explanations may be incomplete.

May 27 ArXiv

Composition Collapse in LLMs

Research demonstrating 'composition collapse' - the phenomenon where LLMs can have stable factual knowledge but fail at compositional reasoning. Shows that factual knowledge retention doesn't guarantee ability to compose facts logically.

May 27 ArXiv

MemFail: LLM Memory Stress Testing

Introduction of MemFail, a framework for stress-testing and identifying failure modes in LLM memory systems. Provides comprehensive testing methodology to evaluate how memory systems fail under various conditions.

May 27 ArXiv

UnityMAS-O: Multi-Agent RL Framework

UnityMAS-O, a general reinforcement learning optimization framework designed for LLM-based multi-agent systems. Provides standardized approach to optimizing cooperative behavior among multiple LLM agents.

May 27 ArXiv

MobileExplorer: On-Device Mobile Agents

MobileExplorer, a system for accelerating on-device inference specifically for mobile GUI agents through online exploration techniques. Addresses computational challenges of running agentic AI on mobile devices.
